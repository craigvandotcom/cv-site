- **Copy strategy:** avoid revenue-first language and hustle-culture positioning; never
  lead with one-size-fits-all promises. Health transformation is the primary entry
  point, not wealth building.
- **Traffic routing ("homepage is death"):** don't rely on the generic homepage as the
  primary traffic destination — route traffic to intentional entry points (newsletter,
  workshop, apply funnel). The homepage exists but is not the conversion path.
- Course-platform decision (2026-01): custom TypeScript system built into the site, not
  an external platform; placeholder-first, launch before content completeness.
